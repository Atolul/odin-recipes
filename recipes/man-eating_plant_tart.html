Man-eating Plant Tart

Description

A tart-like dish created by Senshi. It is salty and tastes like a quiche with an indedible shell.

Ingredients

  • Leftover scorpion broth (200 ml)
  • Slime gelatin (80 grams)
  • Baraselia (3 medium size)
  • Meeroak (5 or 6 medium size)
  • Betan (3 medium size)
  • Salt
  • Pepper (little)

Steps

  1. Cut up Man-Eating Plant Fruit.
  2. Mix it with slime and scorpion soup into a pan.
  3. Cook it.
